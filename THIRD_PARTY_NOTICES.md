# Third-Party Notices

## Internet Speed Reader

The local `Shared/NotchHubNetwork` package adapts passive network-counter logic
from [Internet Speed Reader](https://github.com/Srimi1/Internet-speed-reader).
The source revision, attribution, and changes are documented in that package's
`UPSTREAM.md` and `NOTICE.md`. The upstream Apache-2.0 license is retained in its
`LICENSE` file.

The NotchHub app includes `ThirdParty/InternetSpeedReader-LICENSE.txt` and
`ThirdParty/InternetSpeedReader-NOTICE.txt`. Only passive current-traffic
measurement is integrated; the upstream app is not bundled.
